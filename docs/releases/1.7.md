# Gwent Music Companion 1.7

Version code: 7.

## Fixes

- Schedule safely displays multiple pupil records sharing a lesson ID.
- Message drafts survive rotation and saved-state recreation without leaking into a new draft.
- The composer keeps fields and Send reachable with portrait and landscape keyboards open.
- Notification history and preferences are isolated across account changes. Stale background work cannot publish into a later session.
- Informative secondary text is more readable in both themes, with deterministic contrast checks.
- Message and Schedule tabs expose their selection to accessibility services. Theme options expose their selected state, and inbox rows expose Read or Unread.
- Status and navigation icons follow the app's chosen theme, including live changes and the standalone updater.

## Notification settings after upgrading

Earlier versions did not record which account owned notification settings and
history. Those unowned settings and baselines reset once on upgrade. Check your
notification preferences afterward. The first inbox sync establishes a baseline
instead of announcing existing messages as new.

After this migration, reconnecting the same account preserves its settings and
notification history. Changing account starts fresh; explicitly signing out clears
that account's notification settings. The app theme and updater preferences are
not part of this reset.
